Frequently Asked B2B Sourcing Questions

Are Mitsuboshi and Conti belts supplied in authentic Japanese/German master packing?

Yes. M/s Belubeari Exim distributes 100% genuine Mitsuboshi (Japan) and Continental Conti (Germany) belts with original OEM batch numbers, protective sleeve packaging, and full manufacturer quality traceability.

What are the dispatch timelines from your Masjid Bunder, Mumbai hub?

Orders confirmed by 4:00 PM are packed and handed over to transporters (V-Trans, Patel Roadways, TCI, ARC) by 6:30 PM the same day. Maharashtra & Gujarat clients receive next-day delivery, while North & South destinations take 24–48 hours.

Do you provide 100% Tax Invoices with Input Tax Credit (ITC)?

Yes. Every order is billed with formal GST e-invoices with exact 8-digit HSN codes (e.g. HSN 4010 for transmission belts, HSN 8482 for ball bearings) ensuring 100% seamless ITC input credit claims for your business.

What are the volume wholesale discount slabs?

We offer progressive wholesale slab rates: 5% bulk discount for 20+ pieces, 10% discount for 50+ pieces, and 15% discount for 100+ master carton quantities. You can test these live in our B2B Purchase Order tool above.
